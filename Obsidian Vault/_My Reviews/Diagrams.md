- **Documenting Software Architectures: Views and Beyond 2nd Edition**

Yes, there **are books**—both general and specialized—that **teach how to create these architecture diagrams** effectively. However, no single book covers *all* the diagram types exhaustively. Instead, you’ll find good resources by focusing on **architecture frameworks, diagramming languages**, and **system design practices**.

---

### 📘 **Recommended Books by Topic**

### 🔹 **1. General Software & System Architecture (Multi-diagram Focus)**

These books often include many types of diagrams (data, app, infra, etc.):

- ***Software Architecture: The Hard Parts*** – by Neal Ford, Mark Richards, and others
🔸 Covers diagrams for microservices, data mesh, event-driven, etc.
🔸 Explains trade-offs and styles, with many visuals.
- ***Fundamentals of Software Architecture*** – by Mark Richards & Neal Ford
🔸 Introduces architecture styles and diagramming best practices.
- ***Visual Models for Software Requirements*** – by Joy Beatty & Anthony Chen
🔸 Teaches how to use different models: DFDs, process flows, state diagrams, etc.

---

### 🔹 **2. Enterprise Architecture & High-Level Diagrams**

These books cover frameworks like TOGAF, which prescribe diagram types:

- ***The TOGAF® Standard, Version 10*** (or v9.2) – by The Open Group
🔸 Teaches how to structure enterprise architecture and includes 20+ diagram types.
- ***Enterprise Architecture at Work*** – by Marc Lankhorst (ArchiMate expert)
🔸 Explains how to model business, data, app, and tech layers using ArchiMate.

---

### 🔹 **3. UML & System Design**

Ideal for application-level and process modeling:

- ***UML Distilled*** – by Martin Fowler
🔸 Short and practical guide to creating class diagrams, sequence diagrams, etc.
- ***Designing Software Architectures*** – by Humberto Cervantes & Rick Kazman
🔸 Uses the "Attribute-Driven Design" method; includes quality attributes and diagrams.

---

### 🔹 **4. Data Architecture & Flow**

Focused on data modeling, pipelines, and system integration:

- ***The Data Warehouse Toolkit*** – by Ralph Kimball
🔸 Teaches dimensional modeling with many ER-style and flow diagrams.
- ***Designing Data-Intensive Applications*** – by Martin Kleppmann
🔸 Contains diagrams for streaming, batch, storage systems, etc.
- ***Streaming Systems*** – by Tyler Akidau
🔸 Excellent for real-time architecture and flow diagrams (Beam, Kafka, Flink).

---

### 🔹 **5. Diagramming Tools & Languages**

How to draw and standardize:

- ***The Art of Visualizing Software Architecture*** – by Simon Brown
🔸 Creator of the C4 model. Focuses on how to diagram architecture clearly.
🔸 Teaches *how to use abstraction and visual clarity.*
- ***Archimate® 3.2 Specification*** – by The Open Group
🔸 Defines modeling language for enterprise systems—used in tools like Archi.
