- What is the Problem
	- depends on who are you asking
	- A problem is  a difference between things as desired and things as perceived
- Don't take a solution method for a problem definition
	- If you solve their problem too readily, they will never believe you've solved the real problem
	- You can never be sure you have a correct problem definition, even after the problem is solved. 
- What is the Problem?
	- Each solution is the source of new problems
	- each new point of view will produce a new misfit. Test your definition on a foreigner, a child, or a blind person.
	- Mind your meaning. Alter your problem definition to see if your original definition is precise.
- Whose problem is it?
	- Don't solve others problem when they can solve them themselves
	- If it's theirs problem, make it theirs problem.
	- If a person is in a position to solve a problem, but doesn't have the problem, make something so he does.
	- Try blaming yourself for a change, even for a moment.
	- A little remainder / assistance may be more effective than a complicated solution
- the source of the problem is most often within you.
	- the problem actually comes from the problem solver more than half the time
