[**A Philosophy of Software Design**](https://www.amazon.com/dp/173210221X?ref=blog.pragmaticengineer.com) by John Ousterhout. A concise book that challenged my thinking of what matters for software design.

[**Designing Data-Intensive Applications**](https://dataintensive.net/?ref=blog.pragmaticengineer.com) by Martin Kleppmann. If you want to understand distributed systems from the ground up: this book is what you want to read.

[**Understanding Distributed Systems**](https://understandingdistributed.systems/?ref=blog.pragmaticengineer.com) by Roberto Vitillo. A practical walkthrough on concepts like ailure detection, replication, scalability, resiliency, and operating large systems.

[**Modern Software Engineering**](https://www.davefarley.net/?p=352&ref=blog.pragmaticengineer.com) by Dave Farley. A practical guide on approaches in building quality software.

[**Working Effectively with Legacy Code**](https://www.amazon.com/Working-Effectively-Legacy-Michael-Feathers/dp/0131177052?ref=blog.pragmaticengineer.com) by Michael Feathers. Sooner or later, every engineer needs to deal with legacy code, and this book offers solid guidance.
