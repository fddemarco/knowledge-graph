The Fellowship of Online Machine Learning (FOML) is a loose community of practitioners, researchers, and people in the industry. Our goal is to promote online machine learning through software, research publications, learning material, discussions, and events. We are also here to advise companies looking to do online machine learning in practice.

# ⚙️ Software

## [https://github.com/online-ml/river](https://github.com/online-ml/river)

A Python library for building online machine learning models.

## [https://github.com/online-ml/river-extra](https://github.com/online-ml/river-extra)

A side-car to [https://github.com/online-ml/river](https://github.com/online-ml/river) with a lower barrier of acceptance for contributions.

## [https://github.com/online-ml/light-river](https://github.com/online-ml/light-river)

A Rust library with only a few online learning algorithms and an emphasis on throughput.

## [https://github.com/online-ml/deep-river](https://github.com/online-ml/deep-river)

A side-car to [https://github.com/online-ml/river](https://github.com/online-ml/river) dedicated to online deep learning.

## [https://github.com/online-ml/beaver](https://github.com/online-ml/beaver)

An MLOps tool for deploying and managing online models.

---

Other than the software we develop, we also encourage users to check out [https://github.com/VowpalWabbit/vowpal_wabbit](https://github.com/VowpalWabbit/vowpal_wabbit), which is an excellent battle-tested library. It’s also worth highlighting that [https://github.com/scikit-learn/scikit-learn](https://github.com/scikit-learn/scikit-learn) propose [several](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/computing/scaling_strategies.html#incremental-learning) algorithms which work online.

# 📚 Learning material

Online machine learning can be quite daunting at first. There is a lack of resources to learn from. We attempt to keep track of available resources in a dedicated repository:

[https://github.com/online-ml/awesome-online-machine-learning](https://github.com/online-ml/awesome-online-machine-learning)

We also encourage you to look at [River’s documentation](https://riverml.xyz/), as well as inspect [River’s source code](https://github.com/online-ml/river/tree/main/river). We do our best to write readable code.
