[**On Writing Well**](https://www.amazon.com/Writing-Well-Classic-Guide-Nonfiction/dp/0060891548?ref=blog.pragmaticengineer.com) by William Zinsser. Writing is an undervalued skill for software engineers. Simple and pragmatic suggestions to do this better.

[**Crucial Conversations**](https://cruciallearning.com/crucial-conversations-book/?ref=blog.pragmaticengineer.com) by Kerry Patterson, Joseph Grenny, Ron McMillan, Al Switzler. A helpful resource to prepare for difficult and stressful career conversations.
