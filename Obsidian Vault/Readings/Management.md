## Chapter 1

Great clarification. If you’re now asking about **how to structure an organization into business units or operational units**, then you're stepping into the realm of **organizational design**, **business strategy**, and **management theory**—not data warehousing per se.

Here are the most respected books that **explicitly explain how to structure an organization into business (or operational) units**, including when and why to do it:

---

## 📚 Top Books on Structuring Organizations into Business/Operational Units

### 1. **"Designing Organizations" by Jay R. Galbraith**

- ✅ Focus: Organizational design models, especially the **Galbraith Star Model**.
- 🔍 Explains how to divide companies into units based on **function**, **product**, **geography**, or **customer segment**.
- 🧩 Key Concept: Aligning structure with strategy.
- 💡 Why it’s relevant: Offers practical guidance on **when to separate functions into business units**.

---

### 2. **"Reengineering the Corporation" by Michael Hammer and James Champy**

- ✅ Focus: Business process redesign and its impact on organizational structure.
- 🔍 Emphasizes **process-centric** restructuring over traditional departments.
- 💡 Why it’s relevant: Encourages breaking down siloed structures and rethinking operational units around business goals.

---

### 3. **"The Practice of Management" by Peter Drucker**

- ✅ Focus: Classic foundational management theory.
- 🔍 Introduces the concept of the **“autonomous business unit”**.
- 💡 Why it’s relevant: Drucker was one of the first to formalize the idea of organizing around **markets, customers, and results**, not just functions.

---

### 4. **"Organization Theory: Design and Change" by Gareth R. Jones**

- ✅ Focus: Academic and applied understanding of organizational structures.
- 🔍 Covers how to design structures like **divisional, matrix, functional, and team-based organizations**.
- 💡 Why it’s relevant: Helps explain when it makes sense to structure a business into **units based on region, product line, or customer segment**.

---

### 5. **"Corporate Strategy" by H. Igor Ansoff**

- ✅ Focus: Strategic planning and structuring around different business units.
- 🔍 Introduces the **concept of the Strategic Business Unit (SBU)**.
- 💡 Why it’s relevant: Lays the groundwork for thinking about business units from a **strategic and competitive** perspective.

---

## 🔄 Relation to Data Warehousing (if relevant)

Understanding how an organization is divided into operational or business units **matters a lot in data warehousing**, because:

- Your **data architecture** should reflect the **organizational structure**.
- Each unit might own its own **source systems**, data models, KPIs, and reporting needs.
- Data integration strategies (like Data Marts or Data Mesh) often **mirror business units**.

---

## 📚 Foundational Books on Organizational Structure and Business Units

### 1. **Peter F. Drucker – *****The Practice of Management***** (1954)**

- 🧱 **Foundation of modern management theory**
- 🎯 Introduces the idea of organizing by **objectives and results**
- 💼 Defines **autonomous business units** based on markets, products, and customer needs
- 📖 A must-read for understanding why structure should follow strategy

---

### 2. **Alfred D. Chandler – *****Strategy and Structure***** (1962)**

- 🧠 **Pioneering work** linking corporate strategy to organizational structure
- 📊 Based on historical case studies of major U.S. corporations (DuPont, GM, etc.)
- 💡 Shows how companies evolved into **multi-divisional (M-form) structures**—the basis for business units

---

### 3. **H. Igor Ansoff – *****Corporate Strategy***** (1965)**

- 🔍 Introduces the concept of the **Strategic Business Unit (SBU)**
- 📈 Focuses on aligning organizational structure with diversification strategy
- 📌 Essential for understanding how to design a company that operates across different markets/products

---

### 4. **Jay R. Galbraith – *****Designing Organizations***** (2001, but based on earlier 1970s work)**

- 🌟 Practical application of structure theory
- 🧩 Known for the **Star Model**: Strategy, Structure, Processes, Rewards, People
- 🛠 Explains how to divide organizations into **functional, divisional, matrix**, and **networked units**

---

### 5. **Henry Mintzberg – *****Structure in Fives: Designing Effective Organizations***** (1983)**

- 🔬 Offers a typology of five common organizational structures
- ⚙️ Emphasizes coordination mechanisms and structural building blocks (e.g., operating core, support staff)
- 🧭 Useful for understanding **structural configurations** based on size, age, and environment

---
