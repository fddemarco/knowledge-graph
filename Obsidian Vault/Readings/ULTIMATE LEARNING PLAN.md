## **Phase 1: Problem Solving & Consulting Fundamentals**

*Goal: Develop structured thinking and consulting/problem-solving skills.*

1. **Bulletproof Problem Solving** – Core methodology for structured problem solving.
2. **Cracked It** – Hands-on, case-style problem solving.
3. **Getting Things Done** – Personal productivity framework for developers.
4. **Flawless Consulting** – How to make your expertise influential in team or client settings.
5. **The Trusted Advisor** – Building trust in relationships, useful for cross-functional dev teams.
6. **The Trusted Advisor Fieldbook** – Practical companion to the previous book.
7. **Barking Up the Wrong Tree** – Science-backed insights on success, motivation, and habits.
8. **How Clients Buy** – Understanding decision-making in business context; optional for internal stakeholders.
9. **Managing the Professional Service Firm** – Understanding organizational structures for consulting/tech projects.
10. **Million Dollar Consulting** – Techniques for pitching your value; useful if you consult internally.
11. **The Consulting Bible** – Broad overview; reference for consulting frameworks.
12. **The Secrets of Consulting** – Entertaining practical tips for making your expertise count.

---

## **Phase 2: Strategic Thinking & Competitive Advantage**

*Goal: See the bigger picture, align tech decisions with business strategy.*

13. **Good Strategy, Bad Strategy** – Foundation of high-level strategic thinking.
14. **7 Powers: The Foundations of Business Strategy** – Frameworks for competitive advantage.
15. **Playing to Win: How Strategy Really Works** – Implementing strategy in business decisions.
16. **The Art of Strategy** – Game theory perspective; optional for problem solving and strategic thinking.

---

## **Phase 3: Leadership, Management & Execution**

*Goal: Lead projects, teams, and improve outcomes.*

17. **The Hard Thing About Hard Things** – Realistic insights for tech leaders.
18. **The Captain Class** – Understanding elite leadership traits in teams.
19. **The Goal** – Lean thinking and operational excellence; critical for developers in production systems.
20. **Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion** – Persuade peers, managers, or stakeholders.
21. **Measure What Matters** – Using OKRs to align team execution with business outcomes.

---

## **Phase 4: Team Organization & Engineering Leadership**

*Goal: Grow as a developer into a team lead, manager, or architect.*

22. **The Manager’s Path** – Core guide for technical leadership growth.
23. **Team Topologies** – Structuring engineering teams for efficiency.
24. **Scaling People** – Tactics for team and company growth.
25. **Notes to a Software Team Leader** – Practical advice for managing self-organizing teams.
26. **The Software Architect Elevator** – Bridging technical and business decisions.
27. **Dynamic Reteaming** – Adjusting teams to scale or changing conditions.
28. **Elastic Leadership** – Growing self-organizing teams; complementary to previous.
29. **Illuminate** – Using stories and action to drive team alignment.
30. **The Engineering Executive’s Primer** – Executive-level perspective for tech leaders.

*Goal: Communicate clearly, present data, and influence others.*

32. **Crucial Conversations** – Handling high-stakes discussions.

41. **Slide:ology** – Classic book on designing impactful slides.
42. **Technical Writing for Engineers & Scientists** – Essential for documentation and technical communication.

## **Phase 6: Data Visualization & Storytelling**

### ✅ **Notes on Approach**

- **Sequence is designed to build skill layers**:
    1. Problem solving → 2. Leadership → 3. Communication → 4. Visualization → 5. Strategic awareness.
- **All books are included** but I put optional or niche ones later in the plan.
- You can **parallelize reading** in some phases, e.g., Phase 5 (Communication) and Phase 6 (Visualization).
- Some books serve more as **references** (*Data Visualization handbooks, Consulting Bible, Elastic Leadership*).
