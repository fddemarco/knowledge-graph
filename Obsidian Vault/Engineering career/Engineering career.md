[**The First 90 Days**](https://hbr.org/books/watkins?ref=blog.pragmaticengineer.com) by Michael D. Watkins. The first 3 months at a new company can make or break your next few years there. The best book I’ve found on efficient onboarding – even if it is not specific to software engineers.\

[**Building a Career in Software**](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4842-6147-7?ref=blog.pragmaticengineer.com) by Daniel Heller. Written by my former colleague at Uber: the opinionated and concise advice resonated with me.

[**The Staff Engineer's Path**](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-staff-engineers/9781098118723/?ref=blog.pragmaticengineer.com) by Tanya Reilly. A deeepdive into the increasingly vague staff+ role that we also covered in this book – but not nearly in as much detail as Tanya does. Read [an exerpt from the book here](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-staff-engineers-path?ref=blog.pragmaticengineer.com) or

[**Staff Engineer**](https://staffeng.com/?ref=blog.pragmaticengineer.com) by Will Larson. Another excellent deepdive into what the staff+ role looks like: with real-world stories from 14 staff+ engineers.
